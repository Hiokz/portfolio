About Me

Hello! I'm Selamat — an Electronic & Computer Engineering student with a strong foundation across both hardware and software disciplines. My academic journey has taken me deep into fields like Microcontroller Applications, Electronic System Design, and Computer Networking (Cisco Packet Tracer), giving me a solid grounding in how systems work at their core.

On the software side, I work with Mobile Computing (Flutter & Dart), Application Development & Cloud Services (UWP), Database Design & Application (Python & SQL), Data Analytics & Machine Learning, and Internet & Web Development — bridging the gap between low-level engineering and modern software products.

My ambition is to channel this multidisciplinary background into becoming a Software Engineer — building clean, impactful, and scalable solutions that solve real-world problems.
